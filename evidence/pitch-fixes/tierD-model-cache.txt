=== D2 VERDICT — REVERTED (measured, lost on both counts) ===

D2 was two changes; neither earned its place. Reverted to the honest load/unload default.
The extract.ts try/finally correctness fix (separate commit d4a453b) STAYS.

(1) Vision/OCR residency across verifies (CUSTOS_CACHE_VISION=true):
    - ceiling with vision "resident", delegation off: >=7 (MAX=7, no overflow) — NOT cratered,
      but ALSO no better than the load/unload default (>=6-7). No ceiling benefit.
    - HONESTY VIOLATION (the disqualifier): the SDK evicts the "resident" vision under memory
      pressure (GTE + vision + per-verify Qwen tool-calling don't all fit), then reloads it
      behind my stale cached modelId WITHOUT going through auditLoadModel. Measured in one run:
        my audit vision loadModel rows = 2
        SDK "Loading model" (vision) events = 6
        vision completions = 8
      So the inference log would UNDERCOUNT real vision loads 2-vs-6. For a build whose whole
      differentiator is "every claim matches the log", that alone kills the feature.
      => reverted. The SDK will not hold 3 models resident, so userland caching of vision is a
         lie the log can't back.

(2) Warm-up load+unload at listen() (CUSTOS_WARMUP=true, no residency):
    - first verify after warm-up: 38043ms vs cold call-1 36-41s — NO measurable benefit.
      The weights are already disk-cached (preflight), and Metal state doesn't survive unload,
      so pre-touching them at boot buys nothing. => reverted (not worth the boot time).

NET Tier D: D1 kept (chat residency — real latency win, safe, honest). D2 reverted.
This is Hugo #2 answered by measurement: keep Qwen resident for explain/assist (yes, it helps);
warm-up/cache vision (no — the vision path is the X1 bottleneck AND the SDK won't honor its
residency, so it neither helps the ceiling nor logs honestly).

=== ENVIRONMENT NOTE (demo landmine) ===
After ~3 hours of back-to-back QVAC worker spawns, one vision completion took 99.7s
(state=completed) vs ~13s normal — thermal/memory throttling on the M1. A fresh verify after
a ~75s settle was back to 36.5s. RUNBOOK: do not run a long rehearsal immediately before the
live demo; let the machine cool, and boot fresh. The X1 restart-rule numbers assume a
non-throttled machine.
